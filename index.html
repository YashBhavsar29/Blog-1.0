About

the person holding the pencil

I'm Yash Bhavsar, a Unity game developer from Maharashtra. I started out in a diploma in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and somewhere between the first lab and the first prototype I figured out that what I really like is making things that respond when you touch them.

These days I build games and the systems underneath them: modular, event-driven code that stays readable a month later. I like small tools, clean architecture, and tinkering with hardware when a breadboard is nearby.

Away from the editor you'll find me sketching in a notebook, rewatching Pokémon, and collecting ideas for the next thing to break. This blog is where I write down what I learn from it.
